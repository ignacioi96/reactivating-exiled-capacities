What you'll find here is a living map of some of the exiled capacities that have surfaced while working alongside artists, facilitators, and communities in collective inquiry.

This map emerged from practice, not theory alone. Each cluster is a braid of patterns we've noticed while sensing into what modernity has trained out of our bodies, our relationships, and our collective metabolism. Some capacities were named in multiple artistic invitations. Others appeared only once but landed like a stone in the water, rippling through everything else.

The clusters are not fixed categories—they're invitations to notice.

You might see overlaps, contradictions, or gaps. That's the point. These are porous groupings, not containers. Engage these clusters as you would a conversation in motion—unfinished, relational, and shaped by the conditions you bring to it. They are here to be questioned, re-named, composted, and re-grown.
